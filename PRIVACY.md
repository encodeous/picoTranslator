# Privacy

picoTranslator doesn't collect, store or share any personal data, and has no analytics or tracking.

- **Screenshots:** only the area you select is captured. Its text is read, and the image is deleted right away.
- **Text:** stays in the app while the translator is open, and is cleared when you close it.
- **Translation, text recognition and speech** use Apple's built-in frameworks, covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Settings** (shortcuts and languages) are stored only on your Mac.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/encodeous/picoTranslator/issues).
