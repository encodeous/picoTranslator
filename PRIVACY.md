# Privacy

picoTranslator doesn't collect, store or share any personal data, and has no analytics or tracking.

- **Screenshots:** only the area you select is captured. Its text is read, and the image is deleted as soon as that's done. If reading is interrupted (for example the app quits midway), the image is deleted the next time the app starts.
- **Text:** stays in the app while the translator is open, and is cleared when you close it. Text you copy with the Copy buttons goes to the system clipboard like any other copy.
- **No network:** the app makes no network connections of its own. Translation languages are downloaded by macOS, in System Settings.
- **Translation, text recognition and speech** use Apple's built-in frameworks, covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Settings** (shortcuts and languages) are stored only on your Mac.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/encodeous/picoTranslator/issues).
